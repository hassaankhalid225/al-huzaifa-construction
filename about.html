Who we are

Al Huzaifa Building Contracting is a Dubai contractor working from Ras Al Khor. We build villas and residential buildings and install the MEP systems inside them.

Our team is small on purpose. Every project has one site engineer who answers your calls, signs off each stage and sends you photographs every week. You never need to chase three different subcontractors to find out what is happening on your plot.

We work with the consultant you appoint, or we can recommend one we have worked with before.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Priced line by line

    You get a bill of quantities, not a lump sum. Every variation is priced in writing before work starts.

  • Built to the drawing

    Setting-out is checked against the approved drawings at every slab and every wall.

  • Visible progress

    A weekly report with photos, completed items and next week's plan.

  • Clean handover

    As-built drawings, test certificates and warranty letters in one folder.
