Everything between the plot and the keys.

Four service lines, one site engineer, one schedule.

Villa construction

From excavation to handover of private villas and villa compounds.

  • Structure

    Excavation, foundations, columns, slabs and block work.

  • Envelope

    Waterproofing, plaster, facade cladding and aluminium glazing.

  • Outdoor works

    Boundary walls, gates, swimming pools, pergolas and hardscape.

Building construction

Residential and commercial buildings, G+1 to mid-rise.

  • Shell and core

    Concrete frame, cores and staircases built to the structural drawings.

  • Fit-out packages

    Apartments and common areas finished to the specified standard.

  • Authority inspections

    We prepare the site and documents for each inspection stage.

MEP works

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing, installed in step with the civil work.

  • Electrical

    Conduits, wiring, DBs, lighting and power to the approved load schedule.

  • Plumbing and drainage

    Water supply, drainage, tanks and pumps.

  • Air conditioning

    Ducted and split systems, ventilation and testing.

Renovation and extension

Works on buildings that are already standing, and often occupied.

  • Extensions

    Additional rooms, upper floors and annexes.

  • Structural repair

    Concrete repair, crack injection and strengthening.

  • Refurbishment

    New finishes, bathrooms, kitchens and services upgrades.
