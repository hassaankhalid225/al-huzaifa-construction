Scope

One contractor from slab to handover.

Most delays on a villa happen at the hand-offs: between the structure crew, the MEP team and the finishing trades. We keep all three under one site engineer, so the drawings, the materials and the schedule stay in one place.

  • Villa construction

    Ground-plus villas from foundation to keys, including boundary walls, pools and landscaping works.

  • Building construction

    Residential and commercial buildings with structural, block work and facade packages.

  • MEP works

    Electrical, plumbing, drainage and HVAC installation coordinated with the civil programme.

  • Renovation and extension

    Additions, structural repairs and full refurbishments on occupied properties.
